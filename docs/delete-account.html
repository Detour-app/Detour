Delete your Detour account

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Detour is an open-source Android app (Detour-app/Detour). Signing in is optional: an account exists only on the sync server you signed in to, which is run by whoever gave you its address, not by the developer. Deleting the account removes everything that server holds for you: trips, traces, explored area, saved places, saved and shared routes, badges, friendships, circle and convoy memberships. It cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Tap your avatar in the search field to open You.
  2. Tap Profile & account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The server deletes your account straight away, and the app signs you out and erases that account's copies on the phone.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled Detour or can no longer open it:

  1. Sign in to your server's account page, Keycloak's account console. On a server set up the standard way that is https://<server address>/auth/realms/detour/account; the server's administrator can give you the exact address. There you can see and manage the sign-in Detour uses.
  2. Ask the administrator of that server to delete your account, from the address you have on that sign-in. They remove every row belonging to it with one request, and remove your sign-in from the account console.

If you cannot reach the server's administrator, write to jellemax24@gmail.com.

What is kept

  • Your sign-in (username, email address, password) lives in the server's identity provider, not in Detour's database. An in-app delete tells the server's administrator automatically, if they have set that up, so they can remove it; otherwise ask them. Until it is removed, signing in again starts a new, empty account.
  • An Android backup in your Google account is not touched by deleting the account. See Deleting your data in the privacy policy.

Nothing is kept on the server after the delete, and no copy goes to the developer.
